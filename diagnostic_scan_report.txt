1. Raw model gives strong directional signal (e.g., AAPL BUY=0.574)
2. News engine applies biasing to all three probabilities

4. prob_hold "steals" probability from prob_buy and prob_sell
5. Final prob_buy drops below 0.50 threshold (e.g., 0.448)
6. Signal is classified as BUY (because prob_buy > prob_sell)
7. BUT entry is rejected because prob_buy < 0.50
